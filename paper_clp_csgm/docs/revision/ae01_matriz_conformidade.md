## Phi_lab limits preserved

The conclusion states that the laboratory-porosity supplement scores coordinates not supplied as measurements within each evaluated window on depth-held-out blocks. CLP-CSGM Ridge is lower than G(z_0) in every block-seed pair and has the lower block-mean RMSE on the two shallower blocks. The direct autoencoder is lower on the pooled mean. The supplement is one cored interval and does not establish operational generalization. R1-M11 records that primary per-plug assay ledgers were not found.

## Recommendation

Close AE-01. The scientific concerns are answered by the existing scope statements and comparisons. The few-well and placement limits stay explicit. The geometry caption, the general declaration, and Implementation details now record the confirmed ChatGPT and Grok 4.7 uses. No result was changed.
